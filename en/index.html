Recap

Thank you,
Vilnius.

PROOF took place on Sunday 27 September 2026 at Nova · Kastytis in Vilnius. Over the afternoon, 5 speakers talked, in Lithuanian and in English, about private keys, the “soldier-citizen” in the war in Ukraine, Nostr, CBDCs and how Bitcoin became an alternative to centralized money.

After each block, attendees set up the tools on their own phones, from a password manager and a VPN to a Nostr identity and their first sats.

The evening continued at the Zolak Micro Art Residence, home of the Vilnius Bitcoin meetup. Thanks to everyone who came, to the speakers, to Bringin, the only sponsor of the conference, and to be-BOP, the open-source sales tool that handled ticket payments.

Program

September 27, 2026

5 talks, 5 hands-on workshops, 1 afternoon. Times are indicative.

13:00
Opening
event Doors open + welcome drinks
Meet the community. The real conversations start here.
13:30
Introduction
event Welcome and introduction
Marius Off-Chain
Why PROOF, how the afternoon works, and how to use the book that comes with your ticket.
~13:45
Proof of Privacy
talk Why Your Internet Security Depends on Your Keys
Satoshine · in Lithuanian
A practical look at private keys: the foundation of security and ownership across the internet, not just Bitcoin, and the everyday habits that keep your online life actually secure.
workshop Set up your password manager
Hands-on: install a password manager on your own phone, create a strong main password, save your first logins and turn on two-factor authentication for your email.
~14:45
Proof of Autonomy
talk How the “Soldier-Citizen” Can Help Decentralize the World: Lessons from Russia’s Invasion of Ukraine
Alexis Roussel · in English
A look at the open-source, decentralized technologies emerging from the war in Ukraine (drones, AI, open networks), and the rise of a new kind of citizen: part engineer, part soldier, building the tools of decentralization while defending his country. What decentralized models of social organization could accompany this shift.
workshop Your first VPN, and what it does not do
Hands-on: install a VPN, turn on the kill switch, check for leaks, and understand who can still see what.
talk Rebuilding Social Media Without a Company in Charge using Nostr
Marius Off-Chain · in English
An introduction to Nostr, the decentralized protocol letting anyone own their identity and audience, with no platform able to ban, shadowban, or sell your data.
workshop Create your Nostr identity
Hands-on: create your keys in a Nostr app, back them up safely, follow the speakers and publish your first note.
talk The FreeAI Manifesto: why AI should not be censored or controlled by anyone
Short notice
~16:30
Proof of Work
talk CBDCs, KYC, and the Fight for Monetary Privacy
Linas K. · in Lithuanian
How central bank digital currencies and mandatory identity checks are turning money into a surveillance tool, and what's really at stake when your currency knows who you are.
talk Why Centralized Money Fails: How Bitcoin Became an Alternative
Piktas · in Lithuanian
A look at the problems with centralized monetary policy (inflation, debasement, unaccountable control over your money), and a practical introduction to Bitcoin as an alternative.
workshop Your first sats, live
Hands-on: turn on the wallet inside the Nostr app from the earlier workshop, receive your first sats from the conference, then zap a note and watch the payments arrive on screen.
18:00
Closing
event The real conversations begin
Resources, next steps, community links.
19:00

event PROOF after-party at Zolak
Zolak Micro Art Residence · Vytenio g. 50D, Vilnius
One hour after the closing, everyone moves to Zolak, home of the Vilnius Bitcoin meetup. Pizza and snacks to share, drinks at the bar, payable in bitcoin. Open to all attendees.
