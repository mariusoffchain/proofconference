A Guide to Autonomy, PROOF edition in English and Lithuanian
============================================================

ORIGINAL WORK

A Guide to Autonomy is a learning book about personal and digital autonomy,
privacy and money, written for the first PROOF conference (Vilnius, 2026) and
carried as a community project by Cypher Baltics.

Copyright (C) 2026 the contributors of A Guide to Autonomy.

MODIFICATIONS IN THIS EDITION

This edition was modified on 2026-10-22. The English and Lithuanian Markdown
sources were revised and rendered as static pages in the guide interface,
under /book/en/ and /book/ of proofconference.com, with shared checklist
action IDs, a search index, an offline copy, this notice, a legal page and a
source archive. The exact sources of this version are in the source archive
offered on every page (/book/source/proof-guide-source-2026-10-22.zip).
