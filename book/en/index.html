An initiative of Cypher Baltics

A learning book for everyone about personal and digital autonomy: keeping your conversations and accounts private, choosing the tools you depend on, and holding your own money, including Bitcoin.

Each chapter starts from a concrete need, explains why a tool helps and where it falls short, then walks you through the steps, with sources close to the passages they support.

About this edition

First edition of A Guide to Autonomy, in the identity of the PROOF Conference. The initiative is carried by Cypher Baltics. Later editions are independent: a community can fork the book and adapt its look, layout, navigation, structure, content and code.

No ads and no account. Only the source links you open lead to other sites.
