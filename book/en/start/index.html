Method

How each chapter works

  1. 01The needWhat is at stake, and why it concerns you.
  2. 02Options and limitsTools compared side by side, with what each one asks of you.
  3. 03StepsA step-by-step tutorial for one reasonable setup.
  4. 04SourcesLinked next to the passages they support, and listed at the end.

Each chapter ends with a checklist where every action is tagged with its effort, such as 5 min or 1 hour. Do 1 action at a time. Your ticks and read marks are saved in this browser only, on this device, with no account.

The introduction explains why the book exists and how to use it. Each part opens with a short essay: Proof of Privacy, Proof of Autonomy, Proof of Work. The glossary explains the words that come back. All 90 actions are also gathered on one page.
